Frequently Asked Questions

How does AgriGuard AI classify crop pests?

AgriGuard AI utilizes an EfficientNetB4 deep convolutional neural network trained on thousands of verified agricultural pathology images. The network evaluates foliar shape, color mottling, insect morphology, and feeding damage patterns.

Can I use this without internet in remote fields?

Yes! When published as a Progressive Web App or when using our offline static mode, AgriGuard AI operates using local cached botanical profiles, and local Flask inference can run on lightweight field edge devices without internet access.

How can I submit new pest classes for training?

Send an inquiry using the form on this page selecting "Model Research & Academic Partnership". We collaborate with university extension programs to add regional pest classes and disease phenotypes.

Are the recommended treatments eco-friendly?

Yes. Our guidelines strictly follow Integrated Pest Management (IPM) protocols. We prioritize mechanical removal, biological natural predators, and cold-pressed botanical neem oils before synthetic intervention.
