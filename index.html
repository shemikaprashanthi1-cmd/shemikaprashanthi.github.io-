        Supported Crop Pest Classes

        Our model is trained to distinguish between high-risk sap-feeders, chewing insects, pod borers, and healthy crop foliage.

        Aphids (Greenfly / Blackfly)

        Aphis gossypii / Myzus persicae

        Moderate

        Small sap-sucking insects colonizing young leaves and stems, causing severe curling, sticky honeydew, and transmitting plant viruses.

        🌱 Cotton, Cucurbits, Solanaceous Crops

        Bollworm / Fruit Borer

        Helicoverpa armigera / Pectinophora gossypiella

        Critical

        Destructive caterpillars boring into pods, bolls, and fruits, expelling frass and causing severe economic yield destruction.

        🌱 Cotton, Tomato, Chickpea, Pigeonpea

        Grasshopper / Locust

        Acrididae / Schistocerca spp.

        High

        Chewing pests capable of rapid defoliation and skeletonizing foliage on cereal crops and field boundaries during population surges.

        🌱 Rice, Maize, Sorghum, Millets

        Thrips (Chilli / Onion Thrips)

        Scirtothrips dorsalis / Thrips tabaci

        High

        Slender fringed-wing insects rasping leaf surfaces, resulting in silvery scarring, upward leaf curling, and fatal viral transmission.

        🌱 Chili, Onion, Garlic, Tomato, Cotton

        Whitefly

        Bemisia tabaci

        Critical

        Minute sap-sucking insects that fly in white clouds, producing sooty mold and acting as the primary vector for devastating Begomoviruses.

        🌱 Cotton, Tomato, Cassava, Cucurbits

        Healthy Plant

        No Pest Detected

        Safe

        Vibrant, intact crop foliage with zero insect colonies, feeding punctures, or pathogen symptoms. Continuous field monitoring recommended.

        🌱 All Agricultural Crops

        How AgriGuard AI Identifies Pests

        01

        Image Acquisition

        Field camera captures crop leaves in high definition. Normalized and pre-processed to 256×256 RGB tensor matrix.

        02

        EfficientNetB4 Backbone

        MBConv inverted residual blocks and Squeeze-and-Excitation mechanisms extract intricate foliar patterns and insect silhouettes.

        03

        Softmax Probability

        Global Average Pooling and Softmax activation output normalized statistical distribution across 6 target agricultural classes.

        04

        IPM Prescription

        Immediate delivery of biological, cultural, and chemical controls to minimize crop loss and reduce chemical overuse.
