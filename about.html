AgriGuard AI was created to bridge cutting-edge deep learning research and practical, field-level agronomy. Our mission is to empower farmers with instantaneous, laboratory-grade pest diagnosis to preserve crop yields and drastically reduce indiscriminate chemical usage.

The Challenge We Solve

Globally, pests and plant pathogens destroy between 20% to 40% of agricultural production annually. Smallholder farmers and agronomists frequently face delayed diagnosis, leading to misapplied broad-spectrum pesticides, environmental pollution, beneficial pollinator mortality, and rapid chemical resistance.

AgriGuard AI provides an immediate, accessible first line of defense: take a picture of an afflicted leaf or bug, and receive a diagnosis with targeted biological and organic treatment options in less than 200 milliseconds.

Our Vision for Agriculture

We envision a future of Precision Agriculture for Every Farm, where digital intelligence is not restricted to industrial mega-farms with satellite drones, but available directly in the palms of growers everywhere.

By integrating Integrated Pest Management (IPM) guidelines into automated diagnosis, we replace blanket pesticide spraying with targeted, ecological, and biologically sound crop protection.
